SECUREDLANDING v3.4 — TAX ADMINISTRATION

1. Upload all project files to your repository.
2. In Supabase SQL Editor, run:
   supabase/migrations/20260730_v3_4_tax_admin.sql
3. Confirm the existing v3.3 Tax Center migration has already been run.
4. Deploy through Vercel.
5. Sign in as an administrator and open /admin/tax.

The Tax Reporting Center can now:
- Upload PDF forms to the private tax-documents bucket
- Assign forms to borrowers or investors by user UUID
- Associate forms with a loan or investment
- Search and filter issued documents
- Preview documents through short-lived signed URLs
- Mark forms Pending, Available, Corrected, or Voided
- Record lifecycle changes in tax_document_audit

Expected storage path:
{recipient-user-uuid}/{tax-year}/{generated-file-name}.pdf
