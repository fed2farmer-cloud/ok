SecuredLanding v4.4.4 - Compact Secondary Market and Wallet/NMI Checkout

MODIFIED
- src/pages/SecondaryMarket.tsx
  Added the shared AppLayout so the standard SecuredLanding header and footer render.

  Registered /secondary-market/purchase/:listingId.

NEW
- src/pages/SecondaryCertificateCheckout.tsx
  Added checkout styling aligned with the original loan-purchase payment selector.
  Supports Wallet, Wallet + Card and Card payment choices.
  Automatically selects Wallet when fully funded, Wallet + Card when partially funded,
  and Card when the wallet has no available cash.
  Uses NMI to credit only the required wallet shortfall before the existing atomic
  secondary-market purchase transfers ownership and credits the seller.
  Checks listing availability before any NMI charge and blocks seller self-purchases.
  Prevents duplicate card charges after an approved payment when certificate transfer fails.
  Discloses the existing $10 NMI minimum and leaves any excess in wallet cash.

- SECONDARY_MARKET_PURCHASE_TEST_PLAN_v4_4_4.md
