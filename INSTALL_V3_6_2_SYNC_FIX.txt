WHAT THIS FIXES
- Accepted borrower counteroffers become the authoritative loan amount.
- Marketplace loan_amount and funding_goal synchronize to the accepted amount.
- Existing amount_funded/amount_remaining are rebuilt from actual investments.
- Card payments look up loans by the public six-digit loan number correctly.
- Split Wallet + Card consumes the wallet portion, including a leftover below $100 (example: $35).
- A split/card investment is recorded once for the TOTAL investment amount.
- 7-day investor protection fields are preserved.

EXPECTED FOR THE SCREENSHOTS
